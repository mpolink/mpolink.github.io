# MPOLink

## English

Welcome to MPOLink! Here, you can enjoy MPO games with friends and explore the world of MPO alongside the community.

## Trading Cards

Trading cards are enjoyable collectibles you can acquire by playing games and exploring MPOLink.

### How to get

Get Trading Cards by playing games, exploring MPOLink, talking to Bubbles, or buying custom trading cards.

## Bubbles

Bubbles is your friendly canine assistant that helps you explore MPOLink, suggests what to do, ask you friendly questions, or even advertise anything!

## MCoins

MCoins are like "money" you can use in MPOLink. Get them by playing games, selling items, or making progress on special activites. You start with M:10.00 and you can also get MCoins by talking to Bubbles 10, 20, 30, 50, 100, 300, 500, 1000, 2026, 5000, 10000, 50000, or 100000 times.
